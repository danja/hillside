# Tides

Tides is intended for quiet, spacious, late-night ambient techno.

## Music Brief

- Around 95-115 BPM, with a slow pulse but not sleepy.
- Deep rounded kick, soft sub-bass, and lots of negative space.
- Small delayed percussion clicks, filtered noise, and occasional metallic ticks.
- Warm pads or drones that slowly change harmony.
- No huge drops; more like pressure, drift, and small details emerging from darkness.

## Visualization Concept

Tides is a black ocean-like field of thin contour lines and translucent sediment clouds. The kick sends slow circular tide ripples across the frame. Bass bends the contour field. Pads create broad glowing currents. Percussion clicks release tiny white flecks that drift, fade, and get pulled into eddies.

It should feel calm, deep, precise, and hypnotic rather than explosive.

## Palette

- Near-black background.
- Deep teal.
- Dull gold.
- Smoky violet.
- Occasional off-white pinpoints for percussion detail.

## Audio Mapping

- Bass and kick: circular tide ripples, contour displacement, and slow pressure bends.
- Mids and pads: broad glowing currents and field drift.
- Treble and percussion: tiny flecks, brief glints, and fine line shimmer.
- Quiet sections: the field remains alive through slow currents, but details recede into black.
